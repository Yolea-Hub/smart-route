Accessibility statement

Scope

This statement covers the smartroute.yolea.ai site, in French and in English.

Target standard

WCAG 2.2, level AA. Body text aims at level AAA in the Day theme.

Status: “partially conformant”.

Assessment date: 4 November 2026.

What is checked

  • Automated axe-core 4.14 audit on every application screen, in Day and Night theme, and on the site pages at 1280, 360 and 320 px: 0 violations.
  • Keyboard-only walk-through: focus is visible, it moves to each screen's title, F6 returns from the web panels to the application, Escape closes confirmations.
  • Accessibility tree review: landmarks, names and live regions.
  • Reflow at 320 px without horizontal scrolling.
  • The automatic rules of the Sogoma kit (sogoma-check): words, tokens, motion durations, French and English parity.
  • The animated diagram has a text alternative, and it stays still when your system asks to reduce animations.
  • The site reads without JavaScript: only the choice of download and the animated diagram depend on it.

Known gaps

  • Not done yet: tests with the NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver screen readers; browser zoom at 200 to 400 %.
  • The web pages shown in the panels (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com) are run by third parties: their accessibility is outside our control.
  • The logos of the tool publishers are shown as supplied: their contrast is not ours.

Contact

An obstacle on a page? Write to contact@yolea.ai. Publisher: Yolea, Abidjan.

Date

Statement drawn up on 4 November 2026.
