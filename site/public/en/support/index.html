Support Smart Route

Smart Route is free and open source. It was written in Abidjan. The people who support it decide what comes next.

Why support

  • The program is free and its code is open (Apache 2.0 licence). You can read it, use it and change it.
  • It shows no advertising, asks for no account and sends nothing to Yolea. So it has no source of income other than you.
  • Your support pays for working time, the signing certificates for the installers, and translation work.
  • It reserves no feature for people who pay: everything stays free for everyone. Supporting means choosing what gets built first.

Two ways to support

Chariow

Payment by Mobile Money or by card, in F CFA. You choose the amount, once.

Features to fund

This is what we would like to build. Each feature has an issue on GitHub: add a thumbs-up to say it matters to you, or write what you need there. We start with what is most requested and most backed.

Goals are estimates. If a feature is funded beyond its goal, the surplus goes to the next one. We publish the state of each one here: idea, to fund, funded, in progress, delivered.

Who supports Smart Route

Names appear here only with each person's agreement. To be added or removed, write to contact@yolea.ai.

Other ways

You do not have to pay to help. Reporting a bug, offering a translation, proofreading a text or telling people around you counts as much. The contributing guide explains how.
