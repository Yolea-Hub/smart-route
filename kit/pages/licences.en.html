Sogoma icons

Provisional text. The licence of the Sogoma icons is owner decision 13, still open: this file therefore grants no right until it is taken and dated.

The two options

  • MIT or CC0, if the set is meant to be reused outside Yolea. This is the extension blueprint's recommendation (section 4.8): CC BY forces a credit line in every product that consumes the icons, and the calm doctrine has no room for one.
  • All rights reserved, with the family licence, if the set is a Yolea brand asset.

In both cases

  • The four marks (mark-sun, mark-jar, mark-tree, mark-gate), the logo and the Rafiq faces stay all rights reserved.
  • A third-party brand (WhatsApp, Orange Money, Wave, MTN, Moov) is never redrawn as a Sogoma icon; it is used as supplied, under its owner's rules.
  • No icon is copied from another set: every shape is drawn on the kit's grid (check_icons.py refuses a pasted Lucide file).

What remains

The owner picks option 1 or 2 and dates it; the final text replaces this file and the LICENSE file of the @yolea/sogoma package.
