Privacy

On this site

No cookie, no tracker, no visit statistics, no advertising, no third-party script (the only possible request to a third party is the fallback one described below). Your theme choice stays in your browser; it is sent nowhere.

The download section first reads the list of files (latest.json) from this very site, and the files are downloaded from smartroute.yolea.ai, served by Cloudflare: no request to a third party in that case. Only if that list is missing, the page asks api.github.com instead for the latest published version (no cookie, no sign-in), reads the SHA256SUMS file on github.com when your browser allows it, and the files are then downloaded from GitHub. Those requests go from your browser to GitHub, which handles them under its own policy; we receive nothing from them.

Cloudflare, which serves the site, also keeps its own technical logs. We have not yet checked what they contain or how long they are kept.

In the Smart Route program

Smart Route runs on your computer. Your requests, your results, your history and your settings stay on your computer: Yolea does not receive them, and there is no Yolea account.

The program starts the tools installed on your machine (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity), with your sign-ins. What you send to a tool is handled by its publisher, under your terms with them.

The web version is served by the program itself, at the 127.0.0.1 address of your computer: it cannot be reached from another device.

What we never do

We never sell your personal data and we show no advertising.

Who has access

For this site: Cloudflare, which serves the pages and the files, and GitHub, which publishes the files and serves as the fallback. For the program: nobody at Yolea, since nothing is sent to us.

Your rights

You can write to contact@yolea.ai with any question about your data. Smart Route's data is on your computer: you delete it by deleting the program's data folder.
